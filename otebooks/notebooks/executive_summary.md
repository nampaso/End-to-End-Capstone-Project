# Executive Summary: Duka Electronics H1 2026 Performance & H2 Strategy

## Big Idea
Duka Electronics can grow H2 2026 gross profit without raising total marketing spend by reallocating promotional funds toward retaining lapsed customers in Mombasa and Nakuru, scaling growth in Eldoret, and bundling accessories with high-ticket devices.

## Key Findings
- **Branch Divergence:** Nairobi anchors total volume, Eldoret expanded strongly from Q1 to Q2, and Mombasa and Nakuru experienced a Q2 revenue decline driven by lower repeat order activity.
- **Revenue vs. Margin Mix:** Laptops generate the largest share of revenue from a smaller share of orders, while Accessories deliver high order counts and strong gross profit margins.
- **Loyalty Value:** Gold loyalty members spend statistically significantly more per order than Bronze members (Mann-Whitney U test, p < 0.001).
- **Payment Preference:** M-Pesa is the primary checkout method across branches.

## Recommendations
1. **Mombasa & Nakuru Retention:** Run targeted M-Pesa/SMS repeat-order incentives for Q1 buyers inactive in Q2.
2. **Eldoret Growth Support:** Maintain higher stock availability on fast-moving laptops and monitors and align local promotions with Q2 demand.
3. **Accessory Attach Bundling:** Prompt sales staff to bundle accessories with laptop and smartphone purchases to lift margin per order.

## Business Impact
- Recovering lost repeat orders in Mombasa and Nakuru plus lifting accessory attach rates on device sales targets a 12%+ increase in H2 gross profit on existing branch infrastructure.

## Limitations
- Campaign attribution tags are not present in POS logs, so direct ROI per marketing channel cannot be isolated without adding a campaign source field.
- Historical branch stockout logs were not available to separate supply constraints from demand changes in Q2.
